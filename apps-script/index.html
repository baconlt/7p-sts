🌊 The Seven Presidents

Presidents who shaped Long Branch, NJ

1
Ulysses S. Grant
Made Long Branch his "Summer Capital," spending every summer there from 1869–1885.
2
Rutherford B. Hayes
Continued the tradition of presidential visits to Long Branch after Grant.
3
James A. Garfield
Brought to Long Branch after being shot in 1881, hoping sea air would aid recovery. He died there on September 19, 1881.
4
Chester A. Arthur
Frequented Long Branch during and after his presidency.
5
Benjamin Harrison
Vacationed at the Jersey Shore resort community in the 1890s.
6
William McKinley
Visited Long Branch as part of the continuing presidential tradition.
7
Woodrow Wilson
Maintained a summer presence on the New Jersey Shore, having also served as governor of New Jersey.
